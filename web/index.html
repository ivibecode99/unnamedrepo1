CAPT & CO.

Throw your living hat. Capture creatures. Collect 66 Stars. Chase a very grumpy Baron.

Downloads Pocket Edition

Ready when you are Keyboard + mouse or gamepad. Click to start (browsers need a click before audio can play).

Six kingdoms + airship

Forest, desert, city, ocean, snow and a lava sky — plus a hub airship and the Baron's giant Music Box.

66 Stars

Boss fights, platform towers, hidden nooks, NPC fetch quests, coin goals, shop stars and capture challenges.

Capture anything

18 creatures and objects — frogs, scarabs, penguins, a lava golem, a cannon — each with its own way to move.

All original

Every model, tune and sound is generated in code. No third-party art or audio.
