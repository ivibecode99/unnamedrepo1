# Capt & Co. – Play Guide

## The story in one paragraph
Baron Von Grumblebelt stole every Star – the notes of the song that holds the world together – to power his giant Music Box. You are **Pip**, a sleepy hero with a living, sassy, flying hat named **Capt**. Collect Stars, power the **Airship Hiccup**, and chase the Baron across six kingdoms to his Music Box.

## Moves cheat-sheet
* **Run / jump / triple jump** – Chain jumps the instant you land (running) for 1× → 2× → 3× height.
* **Long jump** – Crouch, then jump while moving.
* **Backflip** – Crouch, then jump standing still.
* **Wall jump** – Fall against a wall holding toward it, press jump to kick off; chain between two walls.
* **Ground pound** – Crouch in the air. Breaks crates and ice, stuns bosses, rattles switches.
* **Dive / roll** – Fast ground speed; dive-hop by jumping out of a dive.
* **Swim** – In water hold jump to rise, crouch to dive. Press jump at the surface to hop out.
* **Hat throw** – Capt flies out, hits enemies, grabs coins, activates things, then comes back. **Jump on Capt** in mid-air for a bounce.
* **Hat spin** – A short spin that hurts nearby enemies; in the air it gives a small hover.

## Capture
Throw Capt at a glowing creature (it has a ring above its head) to possess it. **A** jumps, **Y** uses its ability, **X** releases.
18 creatures: *Hopper* (super jump) · *Buzzer* (flaps to fly) · *Stumpling* (stomp) · *Scarab* (dash) · *Cactoid* (spines) · *Sandworm* (burrow) · *Zipbot* (boost) · *Pigeon* (glide) · *Springbot* (huge bounce) · *Jellyfloat* (float up) · *Pinchy* (pincer) · *Glowfin* (fast swimmer) · *Waddlebelly* (belly slide) · *Yeti Jr.* (smash ice) · *Rolly* (snow-roll) · *Fireflea* (flame dash) · *Lava Golem* (walks through lava) · *Cannon* (aim and launch Pip).
Each kingdom has a **capture challenge Star** that normal moves cannot reach – use its creature.

## Stars (66 total)
Every kingdom has 10: **boss** (or a summit climb in Cinder Skies) · **2 platforming challenges** · **2 hidden Stars** (you'll hear a ping and see sparkles when close) · **2 NPC fetch quests** · **40-coin Star** (spawns near the start) · **a shop Star** (buy it) · **a capture challenge**. The airship holds 3 more and the Music Box holds 2.
Kingdom unlocks: Forest 0 · Sands 5 · Tinkertown 12 · Splashbottom 20 · Frostnip 28 · Cinder Skies 36 · **Music Box 50**.

## Coins and shops
Each kingdom has its own coin (Acorns, Sunbits, Bolts, Pearls, Flakes, Embers). Spend them only in that kingdom's shop: Stars, snacks (full heal), **Heart Snacks** (+1 max health, up to 6), outfits and Capt skins. Dying costs 5 coins.

## Kingdoms
1. **Fernwhistle Forest** – boss *Mossback Mogul* (a very sleepy tree). 
2. **Sizzle Sands** – quicksand, a pyramid and *Madame Scarabella*.
3. **Tinkertown** – clockwork skyscrapers and *Gearhead Gus*.
4. **Splashbottom Bay** – islands, buoys, swimming and *Captain Brinebeard*.
5. **Frostnip Peaks** – slippery ice, ice walls and *Frostbite Frank*.
6. **Cinder Skies** – lava, floating rocks and the Summit of Snort.
7. **Grumblebelt's Music Box** – the gauntlet and the final boss in four phases.

## Boss fights
Bosses cycle: *telegraph → attack → exhausted*. Dodge the attack, then hit the boss while it is exhausted (stomp, hat throw, hat spin or ground pound). Each phase has a different set of attacks (charge, slam shockwave, falling blasts, projectile rings, summons, tentacle spin, laser sweep).

## Save slots
3 slots, saved automatically at checkpoints, Stars and when you return to the airship; *Pause → Save Game* saves any time.
