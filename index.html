How it works

  • Card info: Pokémon (pokemontcg.io), Magic (Scryfall), Yu-Gi-Oh! (YGOPRODeck) – free public databases, called straight from your browser. Sports cards are entered by hand.
  • OCR: Tesseract.js runs in your browser to read the name/number off scans.
  • Grades are an automated estimate of centering, corners, edges and surface. They are not official PSA/BGS/SGC grades.
  • eBay / CollX: they don't allow posting from a no-server site, so the app builds the full listing and opens their listing page.
